Our Story

🌟 Tirth Mart started with a passion for bringing authentic Indian snacks and sweets to every home. What began as a small WhatsApp-based order system in 2024 has grown into a full-featured eCommerce platform — built with love by a team of developers and snack enthusiasts from Gujarat.

We believe that good food brings people together. Whether it's Kurkure for movie night, Kaju Katli for festivals, or a hot Samosa on a rainy evening — Tirth Mart has got you covered. Our promise: Fresh. Fast. Affordable.
